# Coaching content

Taken from Rich's session plans for Block 1, Shape at Restarts (November 2026). The wording is a guide. Rich is relaxed about exact phrases and will refine them over time.

## Spots

Five players: Keeper, Back, Middle, Middle, Up.

## Kit

- Us: blue and yellow striped shirt, blue shorts.
- Our keeper: a different plain colour.
- Them: one plain contrasting colour.
- No club crest.

## Our centre kick

- One Middle takes it on the centre mark.
- Up and the other Middle stand either side of her, about the same distance away, almost on the halfway line and in our half. The first pass goes across, or slightly forward as the friend steps on to it. Never backwards.
- Back stands about halfway between our goal and the halfway line, a little nearer our goal.
- Keeper is in goal.
- First pass: short, to a friend. Never long. The friends either side then take the ball forward.

Why: one girl stays back to defend, and a girl either side gives two easy passes.

## Their centre kick

- Everyone on her spot in our half, at least five yards from the ball.
- Up stands just behind halfway, in the middle, and closes down as soon as the ball moves.
- Middles either side, further forward than Up, almost mirroring their players either side of their kicker. (Changed by Rich, 26th November 2026. Before this they were deeper than Up.)
- Back stands on the line of our penalty box, in the middle, at both centre kicks. This leaves space between her and the front player. (Rich, 26th November 2026.) One spot in front of the line also counts as right in the game (Rich, 27th November 2026).
- Keeper in goal, as before.

## Cue words the girls know

- "Spots!" means find your spot.
- "Short pass to a friend."
- Later blocks: "Spread wide, find a friend" for our goal kick. "Back, wait, GO" for their goal kick.

## Story order

1. Chapter 1: our centre kick. Line up, choose the first pass, watch us score.
2. Chapter 2: their centre kick. Where do we stand? We defend well, the ball goes out over our goal line, and it is our goal kick.
3. Level 3: our goal kick. They go back over halfway. "Spread wide, find a friend": the keeper passes out wide, we play up the side, and our shot goes just wide.
4. Level 4: their goal kick. "Back, wait, GO": we wait in our half, then close them down and win it. It goes off the side for our kick-in.
5. Later, from the agreed storyboard (27th November 2026): our kick-in, their kick-in (1-1 at half-time), half-time page, second-half centre kick, our corner, their corner, a last-minute penalty she scores (2-1), full-time page.

## Our goal kick (level 3, a first guess for Rich to change)

- Keeper takes it from our goal.
- A friend out wide on each side, level with the top corners of our penalty box (one dot further up also counts).
- One friend in the middle of our half.
- Up goes into their half, in the middle.
- First pass: short, out wide. Never a long kick.

## Their goal kick (level 4, a first guess for Rich to change)

- Everyone in our half until the ball is kicked (the rule).
- Up just behind halfway, in the middle.
- Middles either side, a little deeper (or level with Up).
- Back on the penalty box line, keeper in goal.
