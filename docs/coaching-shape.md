# Coaching content

Taken from Rich's session plans for Block 1, Shape at Restarts (November 2026). The wording is a guide. Rich is relaxed about exact phrases and will refine them over time.

## Spots

Five players: Keeper, Back, Middle, Middle, Up.

## Kit

- Us: blue and yellow striped shirt, blue shorts.
- Our keeper: a different plain colour.
- Them: one plain contrasting colour.
- No club crest.

## Our centre kick

- One Middle takes it on the centre mark.
- Up and the other Middle stand either side of her, about the same distance away, level with the ball and in our half.
- Back stands about halfway between our goal and the halfway line, a little nearer our goal.
- Keeper is in goal.
- First pass: short, to a friend. Never long. The friends either side then take the ball forward.

Why: one girl stays back to defend, and a girl either side gives two easy passes.

## Their centre kick

- Everyone on her spot in our half, at least five yards from the ball.
- Up stands just behind halfway, in the middle, and closes down as soon as the ball moves.
- Middles either side, a little deeper.
- Back and Keeper as before.

## Cue words the girls know

- "Spots!" means find your spot.
- "Short pass to a friend."
- Later blocks: "Spread wide, find a friend" for our goal kick. "Back, wait, GO" for their goal kick.

## Story order

1. Chapter 1: our centre kick. Line up, choose the first pass, watch us score.
2. Chapter 2: their centre kick. Where do we stand? We defend well, the ball goes out over our goal line, and it is our goal kick.
3. Chapter 3, not built yet: our goal kick. Later: their goal kick and the retreat line, then kick-ins.
