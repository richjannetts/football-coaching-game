# Phone test checklist

Things only a real iPhone can show. Tick each one and note anything odd.

## Install

- [ ] Open the game link in Safari. The first screen asks a grown-up to add it to the Home Screen.
- [ ] Share, then Add to Home Screen. The football icon and the name "Spots!" appear on the Home Screen.
- [ ] Open it from the icon. It fills the screen with no Safari bars, and nothing hides under the notch or the home bar.
- [ ] The "Before you start" note shows once, then player setup.

## Offline

- [ ] Open the game once with internet, then close it fully (swipe it away).
- [ ] Turn on Airplane Mode and open it again from the icon.
- [ ] Play chapter 1 and chapter 2 all the way through in Airplane Mode.
- [ ] Close and reopen. The player and the footballs won are still there.

## Sound

- [ ] Every instruction is spoken, in an English voice, and shown as words at the top.
- [ ] Sound starts on the first tap after opening the game (the big ball on the start screen).
- [ ] Spoken lines keep coming during the explainers, when no one has tapped. (This is the riskiest one: iPhone may block speech that is not started by a tap.)
- [ ] The referee whistle plays after a wrong answer.
- [ ] Turn the silent switch on. Note whether the voice still plays.
- [ ] Lock the phone mid-chapter, unlock it, then tap "hear it again". The line is spoken again.
- [ ] If any line is silent, the words are still on screen and the game still moves on.

## Touch

- [ ] Tap a player, then tap a spot. She moves there.
- [ ] Drag a player to a spot. She snaps to the nearest spot.
- [ ] Drag a player off the edge of the pitch, or start a drag and rotate the phone. She goes back to where she was, never lost.
- [ ] Tap with two fingers, rest a palm on the screen, tap very fast. Players do not jump or copy.
- [ ] Long press, double tap, pinch and pull down on the pitch. No zoom, no pop-up menu, no page refresh.
- [ ] Turn the phone sideways. The pitch turns and everything still works.
- [ ] Turn on Settings, Accessibility, Motion, Reduce Motion. The game still plays, with less movement.

## Bonus question

- [ ] After each level's sticker, the bonus question pops up in big yellow words and is read out.
- [ ] Each picture glows as its answer is read out. The answers can be tapped once the voice has finished.
- [ ] A right answer: a gold bonus sticker spins in next to the level sticker, and the next-level arrow appears.
- [ ] A wrong answer: no "wrong", no whistle. The right picture glows, the others fade, and the voice explains it.
- [ ] The speaker button under the pictures reads the question out again.
- [ ] The menu shows four sticker spaces: level 1, bonus 1, level 2, bonus 2.

## Android phone (Chrome)

- [ ] Open the game link in Chrome. The install screen shows the Android steps, and maybe an "Install Spots!" button.
- [ ] Install it. The football icon and "Spots!" appear on the Home Screen or in the app list.
- [ ] Open it from the icon. It fills the screen with no Chrome bars.
- [ ] The voice sounds English and, ideally, female. Note the voice if not.
- [ ] Play both levels, then turn on Airplane Mode and play again.

## Game sounds and feel

- [ ] Phone volume up, silent switch off. Every button gives a tiny tick when tapped.
- [ ] Picking up a player gives a soft "pop". Putting her down gives a "tock", and she lands with a little squash.
- [ ] Dragging a player: she floats just above your finger, and the spot she will land on grows yellow.
- [ ] No tick, pop or tock plays while the voice is talking. The voice is never cut off by a sound.
- [ ] A good line-up: two happy notes, confetti, then "Great work!".
- [ ] A wrong line-up: the whistle, and the player who needs to move gives a wobble.
- [ ] The goal: a kick sound, the net ripples, a short fanfare with confetti, then the voice says "Goal!".
- [ ] The sticker arrives with a twinkle and confetti. A right bonus answer gets a bell and more confetti.
- [ ] A locked level wobbles when tapped.
- [ ] Grown-ups area: switch "Game sounds" off. The ticks, cheers and fanfare stop. The whistle and voice stay.
- [ ] Silent switch on: note whether the game sounds still play. (They should, like the voice.)
- [ ] Android only: a tiny buzz when picking up and putting down players.

## Turning the phone

- [ ] In a level, turn the phone sideways, then back upright. The pitch turns back each time.

## With your daughter

- [ ] Can she place players without help after the first go?
- [ ] Do the hints (glowing spots, then "let's do one together") help when she is stuck?
- [ ] Does she stay interested through both levels?
- [ ] Does she understand the bonus question pictures without help?
- [ ] Anything she said that was funny, confusing or keen.

## New look (Saturday morning)

- The logo and the Play button use the chunky letters, not the phone's own.
- Words are easy to read on the blue sky, indoors and outside in daylight.
- Buttons sink a little when pressed and come back up.
- The top of the screen, by the clock, looks right and is not cut off.
- In aeroplane mode, the letters still look the same.

## Mini players

- Each player on the pitch looks like a small copy of the player on the menu, with the same hair and skin.
- A player is easy to pick up with a small finger, by her head or her feet.
- Two players standing close can still be told apart.
- Turn the phone sideways: players stay upright and the bench players do not hide each other.
- The GK badge is readable on both keepers.

## First run: story and team setup

- [ ] In Grown-ups, tap "Delete everything" to start as a new player.
- [ ] After the grown-ups note, three story pages play, each with a picture and the voice. Tap the green arrow to turn each page.
- [ ] On the third page a yellow space with a question mark glows. The arrow then opens the make-your-player screen.
- [ ] After her player, "Your team" shows her team-mates. Tap one to change her, then tap the tick.
- [ ] "The other team" comes next. Choose a shirt style, shirt colour, second colour, shorts and socks. The little player changes each time.
- [ ] The last story page shows her with the team and Coach. The arrow goes to the path.
- [ ] Turn the phone sideways on a story page. The picture and the arrow do not overlap.

## Pitch

- [ ] The ball looks like a real black and white football. Ask your daughter what it is.
- [ ] The goals look a bit bigger, with a net.
- [ ] Level 1 and level 2: put the back player one dot in front of the penalty box line. It counts as right. On the line also counts.

## My team

- [ ] On the menu, tap the white button with five faces and the team name. The team screen opens and the voice explains it.
- [ ] Tap a team-mate. Change her name and look, then tap the tick. She shows the new name and look on the team screen.
- [ ] Type a new name for our team and for the other team. Numbers and symbols do not go in.
- [ ] Change the other team's shirt style, colours, shorts and socks. The little player changes kit each time.
- [ ] Play a level. Each of our players has her name under her feet, the keeper has her name and the GK badge, and the other team wears the kit you chose.
- [ ] The dark strip above the pitch shows both team names.
- [ ] Turn the phone sideways in a level. Names on the bench hide until you pick a player up. Check that still feels all right.
- [ ] Give one player a long name (9 letters or more). On the pitch it is cut short with three dots. Check it does not bump into her neighbour.

## Level path

- [ ] The menu is a winding path. Level 1 is yellow and glows, and your player stands beside it.
- [ ] Tap a white level. It wobbles and the voice says to play the glowing level first.
- [ ] Tap one of the padlocks further up. The voice says more levels are coming soon.
- [ ] Pass level 1 for the first time, answer the bonus question, then tap the arrow. You go back to the path and your player walks up to level 2. Level 1 turns green and the path behind her turns yellow.
- [ ] Drag the path up and down with a finger. It scrolls smoothly and does not drag the whole page.
- [ ] Tap your face at the top left. The team screen opens.
- [ ] Turn the phone sideways on the path. The buttons stay clear of the edges and the path still scrolls.

## Sticker album

- [ ] On the path, tap the book button at the bottom right. The album opens on your team's page, and the voice explains it.
- [ ] A new player starts with an empty album: every space is grey with a number.
- [ ] The first bonus question she gets right wins her own player, sticker 1.
- [ ] Swipe sideways, or tap the arrows, to turn the pages: your team, club colours, world colours, kit bag, shinies. The dots show which page you are on.
- [ ] Finish a level: a football medal spins in. The voice says "Level one done", not "sticker".
- [ ] Get the bonus question right: a new sticker pops up beside the medal and the voice says you have won a sticker. It is in the album afterwards.
- [ ] Get a bonus question right on a level where you have already won its sticker: you get the cheer, but no second sticker.
- [ ] Finish level 2 with level 1 done: a shiny trophy sticker appears, and it is on the Shinies page.
- [ ] Change a team-mate's name or look on the team screen. Her sticker changes too.
- [ ] Turn the phone sideways in the album. All six stickers on a page fit in one row.
